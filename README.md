## 🙋‍♂️ About Me

I'm a third-year B.Tech student in **Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at IIITDM Kurnool**, based in Patna, Bihar.
I like building practical AI systems end to end: computer vision models, voice assistants, and IoT-powered products that solve real problems, especially ones that make technology more accessible.
